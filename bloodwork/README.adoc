== Summary
// tag::bloodwork-summary[]
=== Fundamental bloodwork markers to track
* Complete blood count with differential (pl. pełna morfologia z rozmazem)
* Fasting glucose (pl. glukoza na czczo)
* Fasting insulin (pl. insulina na czczo)
* Complete lipid profile (pl. pełny profil lipidowy)
** Lp(a)
** ApoB
** ApoA1
* Liver panel (pl. panel wątrobowy)
** ALAT
** ASPAT
** Bilirubin (pl. bilirubina)
** Alkaline phosphatase (pl. fosfataza zasadowa)
* Thyroid panel (pl. panel tarczycowy)
** T4
** T3
** TSH
* Electrolytes (pl. elektrolity)
** Potassium (pl. potas)
** Magnesium (pl. magnez)
** Sodium (pl. sód)
** Calcium (pl. wapń)
** Chloride (pl. chlor)
* Ferritin (pl. ferrytyna)
* Homocysteine (pl. homocysteina)
* Vitamin D25 OH (pl. witamina D25 OH)
* CRP (pl. białko C-reaktywne) - inflammation marker
* HbA1c (pl. hemoglobina glikowana)
* Uric acid (pl. kwas moczowy)
* Creatinine (pl. kreatynina)

=== Optimal levels
==== Vitamin D
* 80-90 ng/ml but 60-90 ng/ml is fine link:#vitamin-d1[1] link:#general1[2]

==== Fasting glucose
* 70-99 mg/dl link:#general1[1]

==== Ferritin
* 15-400 ug/dl for men link:#general1[1]
* 10-200 ug/dl for women link:#general1[1]
* Somewhere in the middle is best

==== Homocysteine
* 5-9 umol/l link:#general1[1]
// end::bloodwork-summary[]
