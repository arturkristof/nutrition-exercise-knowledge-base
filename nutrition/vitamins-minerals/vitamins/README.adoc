=== Sources

* link:https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uC_0PXXxGvc[YouTube Video by BraciaRodzen]

=== Summary

* Correct doses of vitamin D for people with deficiency:
** 2.000 IU/day for children < 1 year old
** 4.000 IU/day for children and teenagers 1-18 years old
** 10.000 IU/day for adults
** 20.000-30.000 IU/day for adults with obesity
* When vitamin D reaches optimal levels, keep taking maintenance doses (test blood and figure it out)
* Optimal levels of vitamin D:
** 60-90 ng/ml is fine
** 80-90 ng/ml is optimal
* Symptoms of toxicity appear only when dosing above 50.000 IU daily for weeks or even months
