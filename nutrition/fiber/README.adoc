== Dietary fibre-adapted gut microbiome clears dietary fructose and reverses hepatic steatosis

=== Sources

* link:sources/Dietary_fibre-adapted_gut_microbiome_clears_dietary_fructose_and_reverses_hepatic_steatosis.pdf[Research paper]
* link:https://youtu.be/blNLHsww_Qs?si=o8IRgyHQs60brVOa[YouTube Video by Nick Norwitz MD PhD]
* link:https://staycuriousmetabolism.substack.com/p/the-fiber-that-fights-fat-how-inulin[Article by Nick Norwitz MD PhD]

=== Summary

* Inulin - a specific type of dietary fiber - can reverse the harmful metabolic effects of fructose:
** fatty liver
** body fat
** fasting glucose
** insulin levels
** insulin resistance
* When inulin was given, fructose spillover was dramatically reduced. The liver and colon were protected.
** *Protecting the liver from fructose led to decreased activation of pathways involved in synthesis of new fat* which leads to *removing the breaks on fatty acid oxidation (i.e. fat burning)*
* Inulin immunizes the microbiome and shifts the metabolic fate of fructose away from fat production and toward protein synthesis and antioxidant generation
* Inulin sources:
** Chicory root (Korzeń Cykorii) - 35-57g/100g
** Jerusalem artichoke (Słonecznik bulwiasty) - 16-20g/100g
** Garlic (Czosnek) - 9-16g/100g
** Leeks (Por) - 10-12g/100g
** Asparagus (Szparagi) - 3g/100g
* Eat 10g/day of inulin to get the benefits

== Global, distinctive, and personal changes in molecular and microbial profiles by specific fibers in humans

=== Sources

* link:sources/Global_distinctive_and_personal_changes_in_molecular_and_microbial_profiles_by_specific_fibers_in_humans.pdf[Research paper]
* link:https://youtu.be/5dIGzIxrE44?si=aLSwoxNWjT3E9ELk[YouTube Video by Nick Norwitz MD PhD]
* link:https://staycuriousmetabolism.substack.com/p/fiber-is-good-for-you-is-lazy-science[Article by Nick Norwitz MD PhD]

=== Summary

* Inulin in high doses (20g+/day) can cause liver damage, and long-term high-dose inulin supplementation in mice has led to liver damage and even *liver cancer*
* Arabinoxylan modifies cholesterol metabolism in a clinically meaningful way. AX caused a dose-dependent reduction in LDL-C, with drops of over 20 mg/dL at the highest dose (30g/day).
** A high-protein diet - think chicken, fish, eggs, meat—combined with AX fiber might amplify cholesterol lowering.
* Arabinoxylan sources:
** Psyllium Husk Powder by Now Foods - 50-60g/100g
